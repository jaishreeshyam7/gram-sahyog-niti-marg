🧑‍⚖️ Legal Sathi — Bridging Legal Support for All
🚀 Project Overview
Legal Sathi is a React-based web platform aimed at providing easy, affordable, and localized legal assistance to rural and corporate communities. Our goal is to empower citizens by simplifying access to legal information and professional guidance — all available in Hindi.

🎯 Key Features
📤 Upload Legal Documents
Rural users can upload documents (PDF/DOC).

AI-based extraction and explanation of contents in Hindi.

❓ Legal Q&A and Reply
Users can type their legal questions.

Receive legally formatted replies in regional language (Hindi).

🤝 Lawyer Matching
Automatically connect with the best-suited lawyers based on:

Case domain (e.g., land, family, property)

Language preference

Location proximity

🏢 Corporate Legal Manager
Corporates can post legal issues (IP, contracts, compliance).

Receive curated, expert-backed legal solutions and recommendations.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: React.js, Tailwind CSS

Backend: Node.js, Express.js

Database: MongoDB / Firebase

AI/ML: OpenAI / Custom NLP models for document parsing

Language Translation: Google Translate API or IndicNLP

File Handling: Multer / Firebase Storage

🧠 Future Scope
Mobile app with voice input and legal hotline

Integration with Government Legal Services Authorities (LSAs)

Lawyer onboarding and review mechanism

WhatsApp chatbot integration for rural outreach
